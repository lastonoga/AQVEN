# Code of conduct

AQVEN follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@lastonoga](https://github.com/lastonoga), through
a private [security advisory](https://github.com/lastonoga/AQVEN/security/advisories/new) if it cannot be
said in public. Reports are handled in confidence.
