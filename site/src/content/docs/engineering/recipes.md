title: Recipes
description: Start from a supported workflow pattern, then adapt its types, evidence, limits, and verification to your project.
---

Each recipe names the smallest AQVEN primitives for a real workflow situation. Use its links to reach the entity guides and generated reference before copying fields into a project.

Recipes are starting patterns, not substitute contracts. Define your own input/output types, failure behavior, external-effect boundary, dataset cases, and evaluation before shipping a variant.
