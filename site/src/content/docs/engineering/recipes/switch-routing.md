---
title: Route Requests with a Switch
description: Route one declared decision through exhaustive named branches and return a stable output shape.
---

Produce a controlled enum or union discriminator first. Define one switch case for every supported outcome and give branches the bindings they need. Keep the switch output contract stable even when branch implementations differ. Test every branch and an unexpected input.

Read [Switch](/engineering/switch/) and [Controlled Classifier](/engineering/recipes/controlled-classifier/).
