---
title: Approve a Tool Call
description: Review and decide on a model-requested tool action that an AQVEN agent marked for approval.
---

Tool approval waits appear in **Review** alongside human forms. Select the queue item, inspect the proposed action, evidence, assignee, deadline, and run/node context, then approve or deny it.

This review controls one model-requested tool action. It is different from a human node, where a workflow needs a typed business form. Timeouts and escalation follow the agent’s declared policy. [Tool Approval and Human Review](/engineering/tool-approval/) explains how to define both paths.
