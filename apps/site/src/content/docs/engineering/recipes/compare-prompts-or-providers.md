---
title: Compare Two Prompts or Providers
description: Compare one change at a time on fixed cases and inspect both quality and operational tradeoffs.
---

Keep the inference output type, dataset, and scoring criteria fixed. Change only the prompt variant or provider route. Run the comparison, inspect per-case differences, schema failures, cost, latency, and fallback behavior. Keep the winner only when it meets the required product properties.

Read [Prompt Variants and Fragments](/engineering/prompt-variants/), [Providers](/engineering/providers/), and [Evaluations](/engineering/testing-and-evaluation/).
