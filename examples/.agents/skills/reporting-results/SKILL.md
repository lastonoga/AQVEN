## MUST

- A report opens with the result on real data: the number, the change in percentage points against the
  baseline, and n. Synthetic tests only support it, after it.
- Two or three sentences first; details only when asked. An internal variant or check id only with a plain
  explanation next to it.
- Holdout findings (`FINDINGS.md`) stay apart from `dev` signals; a `dev` signal is never called a finding.
- `FINDINGS.md` is generated by the engine: never edit it. `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md` is yours to write.
- Quote `verdict.text` as it is; never round or retell its numbers.
- An experiment whose question is answered gets `archived: true` in its `experiment.yaml`; it is never deleted.

## Procedure

| # | Step | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | After every series that ends `done`: an entry in `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md` with the date, experiment and series id, the question, `verdict.text` quoted, the numbers with n, what it changes | the entry exists |
| 2 | Sections of the journal: Now (the owner's goal and the stage), What holdout settled (pointing at `FINDINGS.md`), What works (`dev` signals), What does not, How we measure (checks, ground truth, controls), Open questions, Spend | a reader without the conversation knows what is known |
| 3 | A refuted belief is marked with the date and the series, never deleted | the history of decisions stays visible |
| 4 | Report to the owner: two or three sentences with the real-data result, the delta in points and n; then the next step and the spend | the report passes the MUST rules |
| 5 | Autonomy limit: count experiments since the owner's permission; at the limit, report, update the journal and stop | the owner is called in time |
| 6 | After a compaction, read the journal before any action | facts of the summary checked against the journal |
| 7 | An experiment whose question is answered (a holdout verdict, or the owner closed the question): `archived: true` in its `experiment.yaml`, and the journal entry says so | Studio's Research lists only open questions; the folder and its findings stay |

Entry format:

```markdown
### 2026-09-24 · panel_merge_rule · series 0199f1e2-… · holdout

Question: does merging the panel by a two-judge majority alone pick the expected winner as often as the spread rule?
Verdict: "<verdict.text as the series wrote it>"
Numbers: expected winner 0.88 against 0.90 (-2 points, within the 5-point margin), 21% fewer tie-break calls, n = 40 support cases.
Changes: the flow now merges by majority alone; the experiment is archived; cost per correct pick is watched in every later panel experiment.
```

## Pitfalls

| What goes wrong | Do instead |
|---|---|
| Asked for progress in a few sentences with percentages, the first answer opened with a synthetic sweep and mixed ranges from different tests | open with real data: the number, the delta in points, n |
| The owner had to ask for one written conclusion that a person or a model can read outside the conversation | keep `EXPERIMENTS.md` current after every series |
| A compaction summary carried a false rule for hours | the journal is the memory; re-read it after a compaction |
| Answered experiments left among the open ones, so the owner could not tell what was still in question | `archived: true` once the question is answered |
| A good pattern: allowed a set number of experiments with a report at the end, the agent stayed within it and wrote a journal that opens with the main lesson and keeps the one finding apart from `dev` signals | do the same |

## Tools and commands

- Read, Edit and Write for `<package>/EXPERIMENTS.md`; Edit for `archived: true` in `experiments/<id>/experiment.yaml`.
- `aqven` MCP `series_get` for the verdict, the matrix and the spend.
