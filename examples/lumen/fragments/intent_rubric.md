The intent is what the customer needs from support now, not the topic the message opens with: long messages often start with a story, praise, a complaint that is already settled or a side question.
A product that arrived working and then stopped working properly is a defect. Damage in transit, a missing part, a wrong item or a lost parcel is a delivery problem. A request for information with no claim is a question.
Write the reasoning first, then pick the intent and rate your confidence: low confidence is more honest than a confident guess.
